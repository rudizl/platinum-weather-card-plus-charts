A beta of 2.6.1: fixes only, on top of 2.6.0. Nothing changes in your configuration.

**New since `v2.6.1-beta.2`:** sunrise and sunset show the right day at dawn and dusk. For a few minutes around each, both times carried tomorrow's weekday and the sunrise was listed first, although the sunset was still today's. The card now goes by the two times Home Assistant gives: the earlier one comes first, and a time shows its weekday only when it is not today.

- **A rain gauge in another unit than mm/h** (in/h, mm/min, mm/d) is read in mm/h for the icon and the forecast. A gauge in inches made the light-rain icon for heavy rain.
- **Precipitation bars** ([#47](https://github.com/rudizl/platinum-weather-card-plus-charts/issues/47)). The bars are drawn against at least 10 mm (0.4 in), so a little rain is a short bar. A wetter day still reaches the top. `option_precip_chart_min` changes the amount; `0` brings back the old scale.
- **Lightning in miles** ([#38](https://github.com/rudizl/platinum-weather-card-plus-charts/issues/38)). A detector in miles is read in miles: red within 9 mi, amber within 19 mi, a default range of 31 mi.
- **Default icons** ([#40](https://github.com/rudizl/platinum-weather-card-plus-charts/issues/40)). Fog, wind, snow and plain cloud now start at the top of the temperature, like the sun.
- **"Use attribute" for the summary of the days** ([#39](https://github.com/rudizl/platinum-weather-card-plus-charts/issues/39)) now reaches the tooltip of the charts too.
- **Small words.** The hint under a custom icon path is translated, and the README gives the right cloud bands for the icon.

To try it, open the card in the Marketplace, choose **Redownload**, turn on **Show beta versions** and pick `v2.6.1-beta.3`. If you are on a 2.7 beta, this is an older version: it does not have the 2.7 features.
