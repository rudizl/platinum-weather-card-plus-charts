A beta of 2.7.0, on top of the stable [2.6.0](https://github.com/rudizl/platinum-weather-card-plus-charts/releases/tag/v2.6.0).

**New since `v2.7.0-beta.10`:** sunrise and sunset show the right day at dawn and dusk. For a few minutes around each, both times carried tomorrow's weekday and the sunrise was listed first, although the sunset was still today's. The card now goes by the two times Home Assistant gives: the earlier one comes first, and a time shows its weekday only when it is not today.

**Since `v2.7.0-beta.9`:** the local forecast is in two lines. The forecast stays in large type; the sky, the wind and the temperature (or the pressure with Zambretti) follow in a smaller line under it, so a day with many changes no longer fills half the card. In Bulgarian the wind is said as a forecast („вероятно ще се усили“).

**Fixes from two reviews of the code** (since `v2.7.0-beta.9`).
- **Weather effects:** new cards now get the wind (snow in a gale is a blizzard again), a dry rain gauge draws no rain, gauges in in/h or mm/min are read correctly, the observations layout has one effects layer, and a lightning strike older than half an hour no longer flashes.
- **Icons:** if a pack file does not load, the card says so and tries again every 30 seconds, instead of showing no icons. A custom pack stored beside the card is no longer left blank.
- **Air quality:** the warning goes by the mean of the last hour, so one spike from a cheap sensor is not a warning. Readings in mg/m³ are converted.
- **Smaller fixes:** the night cloud cover no longer changes the icon by day, precipitation in cm and more distance units are read correctly.
- **Bulgarian:** the forecasts and warnings use the wording of the national weather service, and a warning with two hazards reads „Оранжев код: значителни валежи и силен вятър“.

**Since `v2.7.0-beta.8`:**
- **What a new card starts with.** A card added from the picker now has the warnings on top, the photoreal pictures, the light weather effects and, when the provider has them, the hours beside the days. Cards you already have keep what they have. The weather effects are no longer marked experimental.
- **Precipitation bars** ([#47](https://github.com/rudizl/platinum-weather-card-plus-charts/issues/47)). The bars are drawn against at least 10 mm (0.4 in), so a little rain is a short bar. `option_precip_chart_min` changes the amount; `0` brings back the old scale.
- **The hint under a custom icon path** is now in the language of the editor.

**Since `v2.7.0-beta.7`:**
- **Air quality** ([#42](https://github.com/rudizl/platinum-weather-card-plus-charts/issues/42)). Two slots, `pm25` and `pm10`, for particulate sensors in µg/m³. The number is coloured by the European Air Quality Index (the EEA's 2024 bands): green for good, up to purple for extremely poor. When the air is poor or worse, a row appears in the warnings. Cheap sensors count fog droplets as dust, so above 90% humidity the row says it may be fog.
- **The UV slot colours only its number.** The icon now matches the other slots.
- **In the editor:** the two sentences under the station altitude no longer run together.

**Since `v2.7.0-beta.6`:**
- **The height of Home Assistant for the local forecast** ([#46](https://github.com/rudizl/platinum-weather-card-plus-charts/issues/46)). Beside an empty station altitude, the editor offers the height Home Assistant knows for your home, taken with one press. It never fills it in on its own: if your barometer already gives the relative pressure (about 1013 hPa on an average day), leave the altitude empty.
- **The `wcc-2` icon pack is gone.** It was the same amCharts drawings as the default pack, with fewer pictures, and needed Weather Chart Card. A configuration that still names it shows the default pack, so nothing breaks.
- **In the editor:** the hints wrap to a second line instead of being cut, and the weather effects are named once.

**Eye candy for the editor** (since `v2.7.0-beta.5`). Each topic of the first screen has its icon on a tile of its own colour, and a section that is switched off is dimmed. The icon pack is chosen by pressing a picture of each pack, the weather effects by a small picture of each setting, and the style of the warnings by the two side by side. Under each row, a hint of what is in it replaces the line of what it was set to. Nothing in your configuration changes.

**The fixes waiting for 2.6.1** (since `v2.7.0-beta.5`). A lightning detector in miles is read in miles, for the warning and for the flashes of the effects ([#38](https://github.com/rudizl/platinum-weather-card-plus-charts/issues/38)); "Use attribute" for the summary of the days reaches the tooltip of the charts ([#39](https://github.com/rudizl/platinum-weather-card-plus-charts/issues/39)); and the big icon of the default pack starts at the top of the temperature for every condition, where windy, snowy, foggy and cloudy icons used to sit lower ([#40](https://github.com/rudizl/platinum-weather-card-plus-charts/issues/40)).

**At night the icon shows the sky the cloud slot shows** (since `v2.7.0-beta.4`). After dark the cloud slot and Sager read the sky from the **Cloud cover entity (night fallback)**, while the icon stayed the provider's, so a card could draw a partly cloudy night beside a cloud cover of 1% and "the sky is clear". With **Measurement corrects the icon** on and a cloud cover entity chosen, the icon at night now follows that entity, in the bands the day uses. A forecast of cloud does not overrule a claim of rain, snow or a storm, and without those two settings nothing changes.

**The icons in four files, not 136** (since `v2.7.0-beta.3`) ([#41](https://github.com/rudizl/platinum-weather-card-plus-charts/issues/41)). A release used to carry a file for every picture. Now each built-in pack is one file beside the card (`icons-animated.js`, `icons-static.js`, `icons-photoreal.js`), and a card loads only the pack it shows; with a pack from a CDN or a path of your own, it loads none. The photoreal pictures are WebPs now, 0.6 MB instead of 2.3 MB, with no difference to see. When a card first shows its pack, the icon can take a moment to appear, once.

If you pointed another card or a custom pack at one of the old files (`/hacsfiles/platinum-weather-card-plus-charts/a-clear-day.svg` and the like), they are not there any more: copy the pictures you use to `/local` and point at that.

**The editor by topic** (since `v2.7.0-beta.2`). The first screen lists the sections, each with its switch, its arrows and one pencil for where it takes its data from. Everything else is on five pages under **Settings by topic**: **Appearance**, **Warnings**, **Sky and rain from sensors**, **Local forecast** and **Card and language**. What a tap, a hold and a double tap do is in **Interactions**, at the bottom, as in the cards of Home Assistant. Global Options and the second button of the sections are gone.

Each row of the first screen also says, in a line under its title, what it is set to: the icon pack and the effects, the warning entity and the range of the lightning, the sensors of the sky, Zambretti or Sager, the days and the hours. The English labels of the editor are in sentence case now, as in Home Assistant, the classic style of the warnings is called *Classic*, and a choice left empty is called *Default*.

Nothing in your configuration changes: every setting keeps its key, and a card set up with 2.6 opens as it was, its settings only shown in other places. The colour of the fire danger waits, greyed, until a fire danger entity is chosen, and the local forecast is no longer called Zambretti, since there is Sager too. If something is hard to find, or on a page where you would not look for it, say so: that is what this beta is for.

**What 2.7.0 adds**

- **Weather effects**: behind the top of the card, or the whole of it, clouds drift with the wind, rain and snow fall, stars come out on a clear night, fog rolls in and a storm flashes. They follow the icon the card settles on, after the rain gauge and the pyranometer have had their say. A new card has the light effects from the start, and the cards you already have stay as they are. `option_effects` (`off`, `subtle` or `full`) changes that, and the effects hold still when your device asks for less motion.
- **The `photoreal` icon pack**: twenty-five pictures, bundled with the card, with skies drawn for the cloud cover that a pyranometer measures.
- **Soft warnings**, the new default: the row is a stripe in the colour of the level that fades out, and a storm far away is a small pill instead of a row. The classic flat bar is still there (`option_warning_style: classic`).
- And a fix: the sky of the effects no longer goes over a warning that is above the overview.

To try it, open the card in the Marketplace, choose **Redownload**, turn on **Show beta versions** and pick `v2.7.0-beta.11`. Going back to 2.6.0 is the same way. If you were on one of the `effects` pre-releases, this is the same line, with 2.6.0 under it, and your settings stay as they are.

All of it is in the [changelog](https://github.com/rudizl/platinum-weather-card-plus-charts/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md), and in the README of this version under *Weather effects*. 1376 tests run the card now.
