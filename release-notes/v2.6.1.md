Hi everyone,

2.6.1 is a small one after the big 2.6.0: fixes only, and nothing changes in your configuration. It is the third beta as you tested it, with no new code since.

**What's fixed**

- **Sunrise and sunset** show the right day at dawn and dusk, in the right order.
- **A rain gauge in in/h, mm/min or mm/d** is read correctly, so heavy rain no longer gets the icon of a drizzle.
- **A lightning detector in miles** is read in miles, and the editor says which unit it uses.
- **"Use attribute"** for the summary of the days now reaches the tooltip of the charts too.
- **The big icons** of the default pack line up with the top of the temperature.
- **The precipitation bars** are drawn against at least 10 mm (0.4 in), so a little rain looks like a little rain. `option_precip_chart_min` sets the amount, and `0` brings back the old scale.

The README is shorter to read now that its reference tables fold away. Everything else is in the [changelog](https://github.com/rudizl/platinum-weather-card-plus-charts/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md).
